Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026
In short: Vínculo stores only what is needed so your family knows you are OK and can help you if you need it: your account, your trusted contacts, your daily “I’m OK” check-ins and, if you use them, your medication reminders. Your location is only checked while the app is open, never in the background. There are no ads, we don’t sell data and we don’t track you across apps. You can delete your account and all your data whenever you like.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is David Vílchez Sánchez, developer of Vínculo (Spain). You can write at any time to vilchi78deia@gmail.com.

What data we process and why

Vínculo needs an account because its purpose is to share information between an older person and their family, on different devices. This is the data it stores:

DataWhat it is used for
Email and passwordCreating your account and signing in. The password is stored encrypted; nobody can see it.
Your nameShowing it to your family in the app and in alerts (“Carmen has pressed the help button”).
Language and time zoneSending notifications, texts and calls in your language, and working out the daily check-in time where you live.
Trusted contacts (name, phone number, relationship)Alerting those people, in order, when there is an alert. You add them yourself; we don’t access your iPhone’s contacts.
Daily “I’m OK” check-ins and alertsKnowing whether you have confirmed you are OK today and, if not, letting your family know. The date and time of each check-in and alert are stored.
Location and phone battery levelShowing your family whether you are at home and how much battery your phone has left and, when you press “I need help”, sending them where you are along with the alert (by text, your contacts get a map link). It is only read while the app is open, with your iOS “while using the app” permission. Vínculo never tracks your location in the background.
Your home locationOnly if you choose to save it, to compare and show “at home” or “away from home”.
Medicines, doses, times and doses takenReminding you to take each dose on time and letting your family see whether it has been taken (PRO feature). This is health data: only members of your family circle can see it.
Device identifier for notificationsSending alerts to your iPhone (including critical alerts, if you allow them).
Subscription statusKnowing whether your account is on the free, PRO or family plan.

The legal basis is providing the service you request by using the app and, for health data (medication), your explicit consent when you enter it. You can withdraw it by deleting those medicines or your account.

Who can see your data

  • Your family circle: the older person and the relatives who join their circle can see that circle’s information. Nobody else.
  • Your trusted contacts only receive the alert itself (a notification, a text or an automated call) and, if you asked for help, your location at that moment; not the rest of your data.
  • The developer does not look at your data unless you ask (for example, to help you with a problem).

Service providers involved

To work, Vínculo relies on these services, which process data only on Vínculo’s behalf and for the purposes described:

  • Supabase (servers in Ireland, EU): database, accounts and the system that sends alerts.
  • Apple: push notifications (Apple Push Notification service) and subscription billing. Vínculo never sees your payment details.
  • RevenueCat (USA): manages subscription status. It receives an internal identifier for your account and the purchase information provided by Apple, not your name or email.
  • Twilio (USA): sends the texts and makes the automated alert calls to your trusted contacts. It receives the contact’s phone number, their name and the alert text.

Where a provider is outside the EU, the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID to open the app, recognition is done by iOS on your own iPhone. Vínculo never receives or stores your face or any biometric data; it only receives a “yes” or “no”.

What Vínculo does not do

  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not sell or rent your data.
  • It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • It does not use third-party analytics tools.
  • It does not track your location in the background.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. If you delete it, your data is permanently erased at that moment, except for anything the law requires to be kept (for example, billing data held by Apple).

How to delete your account

You can delete your account and all your data from within the app, at any time. You can also request it by writing to vilchi78deia@gmail.com. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription: to do that, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, or ask for a copy (portability) by writing to vilchi78deia@gmail.com. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Vínculo is designed for adults and their families. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

An important note

Vínculo helps your family know that you are OK, but it is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (999 or 112 in the UK and Ireland, 911 in the US and Canada, 000 in Australia).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and, if the change is significant, you will be told in the app.
