Vínculo

Know they’re OK, every day

One tap a day to say “I’m OK” and a help button that alerts the family instantly. Independence for your parents, peace of mind for you.

Download on the App Store

How it works

  1. You set it up on their phone in a few minutes. They just need to open the app.
  2. Every day they tap “I’m OK”. If the deadline passes without a check-in, Vínculo alerts the family automatically, even if the phone is switched off.
  3. If they need help, a big button alerts the family straight away, with their location.

Designed for them

Large text, large buttons, simple screens and Face ID sign-in, with no passwords to remember.

Alerts that really get through

Instant notification to the family, which sounds even on silent if you allow critical alerts. If help is requested and nobody responds, by text message too.

Vínculo PRO

A text and an automated phone call on every alert, unlimited trusted contacts, medication reminders, scam alerts and an overview with battery and whether they’re home. Family plan for two people.

Your privacy

Location is only checked while the app is open, never in the background. No ads and we never sell your data.

Vínculo helps your family know that you are OK, but it is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
